## The story

> He proposed. She married his brother.

Six words, one complete story: a proposal, a wedding, and a twist the
reader has to fill in — who was proposed to, and why the brother got
the yes.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that single line, byte-for-byte, to `/output/entry.txt`.
Nothing else is built and nothing runs beyond that write.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

The entry is written to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_DIR`).
